# Relational Geometry — Research Log

---

## 2026-08-31 — Existence

The theory reached a clean definition of existence:

> An entity exists in a detector if incrementing the detector does not destroy it.

This separates existence from instantaneous detection and from invariant form.

This allows the possibility of entities whose relational structure changes while their existence persists.

---

## 2026-08-31 — Detector Invertibility

Identified a major requirement:

> "Arguments are constrained by relations" must be invertible.

If a relation has no admissible arguments, the relation must be pruned.

This prevents arbitrary permeability of the detector to the entire relational structure of Being.

---

## 2026-08-31 — Being Contains No Arguments

Established the distinction:

\[
\text{Being}=\text{relations}
\]

at the primitive level.

Arguments are detector-level realizations.

New arguments may arise when admissible relations instantiate them.

---

## 2026-08-31 — No Primitive Causality

Rejected primitive causality.

The desired primitive architecture is:

\[
\text{simultaneity}
+
\text{admissibility}
\]

with apparent causal succession emerging from detector iteration.

---

## 2026-08-31 — Retrocausal Possibility

Developed a candidate interpretation of retrocausality.

The future does not literally alter the past.

Instead, what appeared to determine a future event may be one member of a larger joint relational class whose admissible members are selected relative to the eventual detector state.

This remains conjectural.

---

## 2026-08-31 — Boolean Oscillator Failure

Attempted to generate oscillation using fixed Boolean arguments and relations.

The system overdetermined itself and tended toward static states.

Conclusion:

A nontrivial oscillator likely requires incomplete projection.

---

## 2026-08-31 — Projection

Identified projection as the missing ingredient in the original detector formulation.

A multidimensional relation may be only partially represented by a lower-dimensional detector.

This allows:

\[
\text{full projection}
\rightarrow
\text{partial projection}
\rightarrow
\text{cancellation/shadow}
\rightarrow
\text{reprojection}.
\]

---

## 2026-08-31 — Shadow

Introduced the provisional distinction between detector-visible relational structure and detector-invisible relational structure.

The invisible component is not automatically absent from Being.

This creates a possible mathematical location for degrees of freedom that influence admissibility without appearing directly in the detector state.

---

## 2026-09-01 — Complex Representation

Observed that Euler's formula provides an immediately recognizable mathematical representation of rotational projection.

However, this should be treated as a potential recovered representation rather than a primitive assumption.

The question is whether the relational geometry independently produces the mathematical structure represented by complex amplitudes.

---

## 2026-09-01 — Current Direction

The immediate objective is no longer simply "make two Boolean objects oscillate."

The objective is:

> Construct the smallest multidimensional relational system in which partial projection and shadow produce a recurrent detector sequence without primitive transition or causality.

The first computational experiment should therefore test the smallest candidate relational geometry rather than the previous Boolean universe.
